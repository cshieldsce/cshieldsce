## Hi, I'm Charlie Shields

I'm a computer engineering student at the University of Denver. I like the layer where hardware meets firmware: CPUs on FPGAs, small ASIC-flow designs, PCBs, and the protocols they speak. Right now I'm an avionics engineering intern, writing bare-metal C for flight hardware.

I graduate in June 2027 and I'm looking for full-time embedded, FPGA/RTL and avionics roles starting July 2027.

**Portfolio:** [cshieldsce.github.io](https://cshieldsce.github.io/) has a write-up for every project, with schematics, scope captures and layouts.
